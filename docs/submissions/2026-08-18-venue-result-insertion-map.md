## Core GLHS systems paper — RIVF 2026

### Primary mechanism result

Use as a concise Results paragraph:

> The implemented governance path enforces declared base-state and disclosure-context continuity at persistence time. In opt-in PostgreSQL 16.14 concurrency tests, both an N=4 same-slot workload and an N=4 unrelated-slot workload produced exactly one atomic winner and three stale rejects, confirming fail-closed profile-global compare-and-transition behavior for the enumerated race conditions. In a clean single-process PostgreSQL service-layer benchmark with 30 repetitions per operation, median latency was 30.967 ms for GST transition, 61.806 ms for THSS snapshot compilation, 7.298 ms for state reconstruction, 10.537 ms for governed-decision reconstruction, and 2.122 ms for audit lookup. These measurements characterize the tested service-layer implementation only; they do not establish HTTP, multi-worker, or production capacity.

### Controlled model-mediated evidence

Use only if the paper has a model-evaluation section and clearly labels the cohort synthetic:

> A prospectively frozen controlled synthetic R4 cohort included 64 independent subjects, eight balanced mechanism strata, and 1,152 pre-declared solver cells. Across both evaluated models, lifecycle, evidence, timeliness, escalation, and all-axes-exact accuracy were 93.06%, 96.79%, 99.22%, 99.83%, and 89.15%, respectively. Strict THSS achieved 63/64 all-axes-exact subjects with Claude and 64/64 with Gemini. Six of ten pre-registered strict-THSS-versus-comparator tests remained Holm-significant: for Claude, differences versus Naive RAG, LWW, full history, and long context were +32.81, +25.00, +18.75, and +14.06 percentage points; for Gemini, differences versus Naive RAG and LWW were +25.00 percentage points. BTSA was not significant for Claude, and Gemini strict THSS tied BTSA, full history, and long context. The pre-declared non-tied-pair power target was not met, so these are controlled synthetic mechanism results rather than fully powered confirmatory or clinical evidence.

### Mandatory null-result qualifier

If the paper makes broad model-superiority claims, add:

> A later sealed 384-subject Claude-only synthetic run comparing strict THSS with full authorized history did not show superiority (70 wins, 73 losses, 241 ties; mean effect −0.0078; 95% bootstrap CI −0.0677 to 0.0521; exact sign-test p=0.867). We therefore do not claim universal model-level superiority of strict THSS over complete authorized history.
