# Retrospective temperature smoothing with matched flank information

Development follow-up. The temperature kernel in parent commit a8744b2e is the primary comparison. Use its 174 outage keys, 24 observed temperatures on each side, days and times and no internal load measurements. Fit each station separately. Measure the original macro MAE and four-week paired bootstrap over the same 29 scoring weeks. The already inspected masks cannot confirm a new publication claim.

Use the Gaussian conditional mean for a local linear trend with daily stochastic harmonic components and measurement noise. State equations in hourly units are level[t+1] = level[t] + slope[t] + innovation and slope[t+1] = slope[t] + innovation. Two independent rotating cosine/sine pairs have periods 24 and 12, and stationary covariance proportional to exp(-abs(lag)/36) times cosine of the lag angle. Model the training-only clock mean separately. In units of the training deseasonalized first-difference variance (floor 1e-8), put independent level and slope prior variances at 1e4 and 1, level innovation variance at 1, slope innovation variance in {0, .01, .1}, daily harmonic variance in {0, 1, 10} (half this for the 12-hour term), measurement noise variance in {.1, 1, 4}. Select one of 27 settings by chronological validation MAE per station and length. Solve the complete observed marginal covariance system directly. Check that the trend innovation covariance reconstructed with first and integrated second-difference shock responses equals propagation under the two-state transition matrix; compare full predicted gap temperatures against an independently implemented forward Kalman filter and RTS backward pass with the missing updates skipped. Require maximum absolute difference below 1e-6 on deterministic synthetic tests.

To guard against a poorly chosen structural family, add a stationary AR smoother fitted by Yule-Walker on training-only deseasonalized observations, with lag order in {1,6,12,24,48}, normalized autocovariance diagonal regularization in {0,.01,.1}. Form the covariance of missing and observed observations with measurement delegated to AR innovations. Fit finite autocovariances and apply the Yule-Walker recurrence beyond fitted lags. Preserve failures. Select within each family and also select the best family on validation only. Every arm may use both flanks. The family selection has a larger validation search than the previously reported nine-setting kernel. Disclose it explicitly.

Use the same last-quarter chronological validation starts as the kernel. Learn hourly clock and AR parameters strictly before the first validation flank less 168 additional hours. Refit selected models on the first 72 complete weeks, then score registered masks. Retain separate family outcomes, selection scores and both directions of disagreement with the kernel. A candidate win requires 5% lower aggregate MAE than every comparator plus positive paired intervals. Neither Kalman smoothing, structural seasonal time series nor autoregressive interpolation is a novelty claim.
