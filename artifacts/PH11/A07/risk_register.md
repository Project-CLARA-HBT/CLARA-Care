# Risk Register — PH11 A07 (Mobile Consented FHIR Share Integration)

| Risk ID | Description | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RISK-PH11-A07-01 | Unconsented export of personal health data via mobile FHIR share | Critical | Low | Gated by `ensure_medical_disclaimer_consent()` raising fail-closed HTTP 428 Precondition Required if consent is missing or revoked. Verified by `test_consent_gating_unconsented_rejected_with_428` and `test_consent_revocation_invalidates_export`. | Mitigated |
| RISK-PH11-A07-02 | Insecure Direct Object Reference (IDOR) allowing mobile clients to exfiltrate other users' FHIR records | Critical | Low | Object-level authorization enforced by `resolve_profile_scope()`; mismatched or unauthorized profiles return HTTP 404 `scope_forbidden`. Verified by `test_cross_user_idor_blocked_with_404`. | Mitigated |
| RISK-PH11-A07-03 | Administrative role bypassing patient PHI privacy boundaries | High | Low | Administrative role specifically denied in `ProfileAccessPolicy.administrative`; requires separate audited break-glass flow. Verified by `test_admin_direct_access_blocked_without_break_glass`. | Mitigated |
| RISK-PH11-A07-04 | Delegate accessing ungranted clinical data classes (e.g. medications without authorization) | High | Low | Subset check in `_fhir_snapshot()` requires all requested data classes to be explicitly present in `scope.allowed_data_classes`. Verified by `test_family_delegate_missing_dataclass_rejected`. | Mitigated |
| RISK-PH11-A07-05 | Exporting unconfirmed, draft, or hallucinated clinical observations or medications | High | Low | Snapshot builders filter on `truth_state == 'confirmed'`. Drafts remain untrusted and omitted. Verified by `test_draft_and_unconfirmed_records_excluded`. | Mitigated |
| RISK-PH11-A07-06 | Overclaiming international patient summary (IPS) conformance or generic FHIR server capability | Medium | Low | Anti-overclaim guard returns HTTP 409 `ips_conformance_not_approved` on `/export/ips`, and conformance boundary declares `general_fhir_server: False`. Verified by `test_ips_overclaim_protection_returns_409` and `test_conformance_statement_declares_non_general_server`. | Mitigated |
| RISK-PH11-A07-07 | Excessive demographic disclosure violating data minimization | Medium | Low | If `demographics` is not explicitly requested in `include`, Patient resource strips `name`, `birthDate`, and `gender`. Verified by `test_minimum_necessary_demographic_minimization`. | Mitigated |
