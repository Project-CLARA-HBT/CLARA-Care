# Risk Register — PH13 A04 (Wearable Daily Aggregates Feature Extraction and Trend Analysis)

| Risk ID | Description | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation | Status |
|---------|-------------|----------|------------|------------|--------|
| RISK-PH13-A04-01 | Spurious health trends caused by consumer device hardware/vendor migration | High | Medium | Implemented source-switch critic in `detect_wearable_trends`. When primary contributing origin changes across baseline and evaluation windows, the trend is explicitly categorized as `source_switch_artifact`, suppressing alarmist physiological shift warnings. | Mitigated |
| RISK-PH13-A04-02 | Standalone clinical diagnosis or medication alteration from wearable readings | Critical | Low | Hard regulatory invariants: `corroboration_required` is locked to True on all trend results; strict bilingual Vietnamese/English non-diagnostic disclaimers are mandated and emitted on every response. | Mitigated |
| RISK-PH13-A04-03 | Double counting of cumulative physical steps from multi-app sync (e.g. Health Connect + Samsung) | High | Medium | Deduplication engine groups by origin and date. Multi-origin summation is prohibited; system deterministically selects preferred origin or highest coverage origin. | Mitigated |
| RISK-PH13-A04-04 | Physiologically impossible sensor artifacts skewing personal baseline or trends | High | Low | Hard physiological boundary guards: resting HR < 30 or > 220 bpm quarantined; daily steps > 100,000 quarantined; sleep duration > 24h quarantined. Non-parametric Theil-Sen slope and MAD resist single-day outliers. | Mitigated |
| RISK-PH13-A04-05 | Cross-patient profile data contamination in aggregation pipelines | Critical | Low | Input validation strictly enforces identical `person_key` across all records in an aggregation batch, immediately throwing `WearableValidationError` on cross-profile contamination. | Mitigated |
| RISK-PH13-A04-06 | Absence of continuous wearable data misconstrued as normal finding or zero metric | Medium | Low | Partial-day coverage (<10h steps, <3 HR samples, <3h sleep) explicitly marks `is_partial = True`. Missing days are preserved as gaps and never zero-filled. Insufficient window days return `insufficient_data`. | Mitigated |
| RISK-PH13-A04-07 | Premature production activation before clinical and privacy verification | Medium | Low | Feature is gated behind `wearable_aggregates_enabled = False` in `services/ml/src/clara_ml/config.py`. Endpoints return HTTP 404 when disabled. | Mitigated |
