# Risk Register — Subagent A05 (Web UX)

| Risk ID | Risk Category | Severity | Likelihood | Impact Description | Path:Line Anchor | Mitigation / Remediation Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RSK-A05-041** | Cross-Tenant Client Cache Contamination | CRITICAL | HIGH | When a clinician or tenant administrator switches from Organization A (e.g., Hospital 1) to Organization B (e.g., Clinic 2), client-side queries or cached patient rosters could bleed across tenant boundaries if not purged synchronously. | `apps/web/lib/organization-context.ts:75-95` | Synchronously execute `clearOrganizationScopedClientState()` in `setActiveOrganizationId` before updating context, purging all `clara_org_cache:*` and `clara_tenant_cache:*` keys. Dispatch `clara:organization-context-changed` and invoke `router.refresh()`. |
| **RSK-A05-042** | Client-Side IDOR & Header Manipulation | HIGH | MEDIUM | An attacker or malicious user could manipulate `X-CLARA-Organization-Id` in localStorage or request headers to attempt unauthorized access to another tenant's records. | `apps/web/lib/http-client.ts:285-296`, `apps/web/lib/api/v2-client.ts:1511-1520` | Document and enforce that `X-CLARA-Organization-Id` is strictly a presentation/cache partition hint. Backend API gateway independently checks JWT claims and organization membership tables, returning uniform HTTP 404 (non-enumeration) on unauthorized resources. |
| **RSK-A05-043** | Clinical Custody & CCHN Misattribution | HIGH | LOW | Displaying patient charts without verified clinician CCHN licensing and facility custody creates clinical ambiguity and violates Vietnam Medical Law No. 15/2023/QH15. | `apps/web/components/organization/organization-link-badge.tsx:25-70`, `apps/web/components/clinical/patient-roster.tsx:1220-1234` | Implement `OrganizationLinkBadge` in full variant across patient dossiers, rendering attending physician name, verified CCHN practitioner certificate number, facility tier, and EMR integration state. |
| **RSK-A05-044** | Workspace vs Organization Semantic Confusion | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | Users may confuse role-based persona switching (`WorkspaceSwitcher`: Personal, Clinical, Research, Admin) with multi-tenant healthcare facility switching (`OrganizationSwitcher`). | `apps/web/components/shell/workspace-switcher.tsx:48-93`, `apps/web/components/organization/organization-switcher.tsx:40-75` | Explicitly separate the components: `WorkspaceSwitcher` manages global system navigation and role modes; `OrganizationSwitcher` manages healthcare facility boundaries with dedicated tier badges ("Bệnh viện", "Phòng khám", "Cơ sở Y tế") and facility icons. |
| **RSK-A05-045** | Single-Org & Consumer Degradation | LOW | LOW | Consumer users or clinicians belonging to only a single organization experiencing broken UI or clutter if multi-tenant controls are forced. | `apps/web/components/organization/organization-switcher.tsx:95-125` | `OrganizationSwitcher` degrades cleanly: displays an independent/personal pill when 0 orgs exist, a clean facility badge without dropdown when 1 org exists, and activates the interactive menu only when `organizations.length > 1`. |
