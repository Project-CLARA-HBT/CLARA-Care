# CareGuard-VN literature lock

Status: **claim constraint, not a completed systematic review**.

The study must treat RxMap (2026), the 2026 medical-concept-normalization
survey, selective-prediction literature, and CrossDDI (BioNLP 2026) as close
prior work. Before source acquisition and evaluation, the permitted claim is
limited to **Source-Bound Medication Identity (SBMI)**: an identity must carry
a current stable identifier bound to its source and version before a downstream
DDI system may issue a completed reassuring conclusion.

The following are prohibited until a literature matrix supports a narrower,
qualified statement: novelty claims for medication normalization, LLM parsing,
human review/abstention, evidence-grounded DDI verification, or generic DDI
benchmark design. Historical DrugBank conformance remains outside the
independent-evidence endpoint.
