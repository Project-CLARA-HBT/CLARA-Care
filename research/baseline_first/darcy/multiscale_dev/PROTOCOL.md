# Frozen development protocol

Use observed 16-grid pressure to predict the 32-grid pressure at missing coordinates. Fit training fields 0–511 and score 512–639, preserving all later indices. Use nearest extension beyond the last coarse coordinate. Compare bilinear and cubic spline interpolation with separate least-squares residual regressions for each missing parity. Each regression uses four corner pressures minus the bilinear estimate and four interactions of those differences with a centered binary gate. The experimental gate is the corner coefficient, the pressure-only control gate is corner pressure above the four-corner mean. Center gates by subtracting their corner mean. Fit without intercept or tuning. Report per-field missing-cell relative L2 and mean across development fields. Verify same-index restriction of both inputs and exact observed predictions. Use only named training members extracted from the archives.
