# Track 6: preliminary provenance screen

Status: **incomplete; no performance or feasibility conclusion**. No holdout was accessed. No authentic development coefficients were obtained, and no solver, warm start, interface control, or matched-accuracy timing was run. Consequently the 20% gate cannot be evaluated and this is not evidence that the proposed mechanism wins or loses. The environment is native Linux, not WSL.

The upstream PDEBench repository contains a misleadingly relevant script, [`run_DarcyFlow2D.sh`](https://github.com/pdebench/PDEBench/blob/main/pdebench/data_gen/data_gen_NLE/ReactionDiffusionEq/run_DarcyFlow2D.sh), which invokes `reaction_diffusion_2D_multi_solution_Hydra.py`. That generator explicitly evolves a time-dependent field, applies `bc_2D(..., mode="Neumann")`, and saves `2D_ReacDiff` arrays and a diffusion coefficient ([source](https://github.com/pdebench/PDEBench/blob/main/pdebench/data_gen/data_gen_NLE/ReactionDiffusionEq/reaction_diffusion_2D_multi_solution_Hydra.py)). Its filename is therefore insufficient evidence for the forcing, boundary conditions, or discretization of the released PDEBench Darcy data. This observation does **not** establish that the released Darcy data itself is unsuitable; the mapping between that file and the generator has not been verified.

Before any speed claim, obtain the actual development HDF5 and its authoritative provenance, inspect its coordinates and fields, and independently reconstruct the operator and compare residual and solution with the published reference. If those checks cannot identify a consistent forced elliptic problem, explicitly mark the registered problem unsupported rather than timing a substitute. Then compare conventional AMG and Krylov+AMG, a development-only trained or provenance-verified operator warm start, and the interface correction against a same-information local smoother, including setup and transfer costs and failures. Keep the independent RPB source closed until the development protocol is locked.
