## From Longitudinal Context to Safe Action: A Co-Versioned Governance Contract for Persistent Health AI

**Background:** Persistent health-AI systems may receive an authorized record context and later return a proposed update after the record, consent, or policy conditions have changed.

**Methods:** We describe a governed design in which a task- and purpose-bounded health-state snapshot (THSS) is disclosed to an AI. A later proposal remains linked to that disclosure context, while the trusted governed state transition (GST) rechecks current state and governance conditions before persistence.

**Results:** Quantitative results are intentionally omitted from this preparation draft until policy clearance and verification of which existing, supported GLHS evidence may be presented.

**Conclusion:** The contract is intended to reject stale or governance-invalid persistent proposals. It does not establish diagnostic performance, clinical benefit, medical-device status, or regulatory compliance.

Keywords: health AI; governance; longitudinal records; consent; provenance
