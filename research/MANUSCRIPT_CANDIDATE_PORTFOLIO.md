# Eleven manuscript candidates for CLARA

Decision date 2026-11-21. Start with **PolicyTrace**, **optimistic admission**, and **future disclosure**. Each has an executable comparison that can already lose to a credible baseline. The three studies need, respectively, an independently authored temporal policy corpus, a held-out workload with independently specified dependencies, and an observed dispatch workload with independently labeled revocations. None of the eleven has established superiority on its proposed external primary test.

The first nine entries map to the [venue2027 designs](venue2027/README.md). Each links its registered estimands. CareGuard is a separately registered, blocked study. The Vietnamese speech study is a new proposal. Venue ranks below are conference ranks from the official ICORE 2026 records, checked by matching the acronym, ranking source and rank on the portal. They describe target selectivity, not acceptance forecasts, open calls or journal quartiles. SACMAT and PST both rank C, so their original destinations are outside the requested range. [SACMAT record](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/1775/) · [PST record](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/127/)

## Candidate studies

### 1. PolicyTrace — Can temporal tests distinguish translation errors from policy disagreements?

**ICST · A.** A portable test oracle, cross-engine validity and equal-budget testing fit a software testing venue. The registered multiplicity-primary endpoint is the RQ2 mutation score at fixed oracle-call budget. RQ1 measures detection of conformance divergences on paired history/request units. Report unsupported semantics separately. Compare the temporal generator with stateful property-based generation and Xu et al.’s coverage-based XACML testing (SACMAT 2018), with the same oracle-call budget. A port of the latter needs fidelity tests. Cedar, OpenFGA and OPA are systems under test. [ICST rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/1221/) · [Registered estimands](venue2027/sacmat/rq_and_estimands.yaml) · [Xu et al.](https://doi.org/10.1145/3205977.3205979)

**Evidence and decision.** On 24 real Cedar fixtures, honoring the declared fixture format gave 78/78 expected decisions. The legacy adapter had eight disagreements and two errors. That is direct evidence that adapter bugs can masquerade as findings. On one published temporal-access model, native OpenFGA and its OPA translation each reproduced seven upstream assertions and six additional exact-expiry controls. Treat the model as one external fixture. [Query-level results](venue2027/proof/artifacts/sacmat_external_temporal_parity_dev.json) Freeze upstream policy models with independently labeled ordered changes and require portable semantics before counting a divergence. A result confined to corrected parsing belongs in the adapter documentation. GovMut owns its W8 mutant scores. This paper needs fresh cross-engine histories and must survive removal of every W8 case. [External fixture result](venue2027/proof/artifacts/sacmat_cedar_external_dev.json) · [Temporal development corpus](venue2027/proof/artifacts/sacmat_temporal_corpus.json) · [Upstream OpenFGA sample stores](https://github.com/openfga/sample-stores)

### 2. Dependency-complete optimistic admission — When does a smaller read set help?

**COMPSAC · B.** A study of deriving application dependencies and measuring their cost fits software systems engineering. Measure false-stale rejection among oracle-allowed writes, conditional on the registered forbidden-commit safety gate. Compare against native PostgreSQL SERIALIZABLE executing all required predicates, global-version OCC and a clearly labeled Silo-style port. Silo is Tu et al. (SOSP 2013). PostgreSQL SSI is Ports and Grittner (PVLDB 2012). Use the publisher PDF below because the DOI in the local COMPSAC comparator matrix points to a different paper. [COMPSAC rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/871/) · [Registered margins and units](venue2027/compsac/rq_and_estimands.yaml) · [Silo](https://doi.org/10.1145/2517349.2522713) · [PostgreSQL SSI](https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol5/p1850_danrkports_vldb2012.pdf)

**Evidence and decision.** Real PostgreSQL 16.4 development rows compare lock strategies and SSI on shared dependencies. The inputs are local. Adapt a held-out transaction mix from CMU's BenchBase and have a separate specification derive its legal read sets. Report which added clinical predicates are synthetic. Freeze before runtime tuning. Require zero forbidden commits with the registered upper bound, then evaluate the prespecified ten-percentage-point false-stale reduction. Compare cost against SSI even if coarse OCC loses. GLHS already owns co-versioned commit safety. A speedup over an unnecessarily exclusive lock alone cannot support the new paper. [Five development rounds and seed-level results](venue2027/compsac/submission/pg_development_results.md) · [BenchBase](https://github.com/cmu-db/benchbase)

### 3. Future disclosure after revocation — Can derived caches match full recomputation at lower cost?

**PETS · A.** Privacy of future dispatches is the claim. Count independent subject histories with any forbidden attribute observed at egress after revocation. Compare transitive invalidation with a coarse purge and freshly recomputed authorized projection at every dispatch. Pang et al.’s Zanzibar (USENIX ATC 2019) supplies the consistency model for a further tagged-cache comparison. OpenFGA is an independent Zanzibar-inspired implementation. Cache tagging remains an adaptation. [PETS rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/1442/) · [Registered estimands](venue2027/popets/rq_and_estimands.yaml) · [Zanzibar](https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc19/presentation/pang)

**Evidence and decision.** The 12-case in-process pilot records incomplete arms and unverified semantic parity. A paired dispatch study subsequently observed all 185 requests per arm across nine histories. Three histories experienced revocation. In these histories both arms delivered all 384 authorized bytes and zero forbidden bytes. The reverse-index microbenchmark measures invalidation cost. [Observed dispatches](../artifacts/venue2027/popets/dispatch-dev-01/RESULTS.md) LongMemEval (Wu et al., ICLR 2025) offers independent longitudinal conversation structure for a proposed holdout. It lacks the required consent ground truth. Freeze a separate permission overlay, identify synthetic labels, and compare observed outgoing bytes and end-to-end cost, including index construction, on the same histories. If full refresh is equally private and inexpensive, retire the mechanism advantage. GLHS commit acceptance and historical provider erasure are outside this endpoint. [Pilot](venue2027/proof/artifacts/popets_pilot_report.json) · [Index measurements](venue2027/proof/artifacts/popets_scope_index_development.json) · [LongMemEval](https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval)

**External development result.** Across 128 dialogues from one Schema-Guided Dialogue training shard, all three arms delivered every authorized field and zero forbidden bytes under a synthetic midpoint withdrawal. B4 served 305 more authorized hits than B1 (74.55% versus 67.82%), but median process wall time was 159.9 ms for B4, 110.9 ms for B1 and 105.7 ms for B2. This supports selective cache retention for repeated state reads. Cheap local reconstruction favored B2 on elapsed time. The longitudinal holdout and registered density comparison remain open. [Paired external replay and limitations](../artifacts/venue2027/popets/sgd-dev-01/RESULTS.md)

### 4. Recoverable safety journals — Can recovery reconcile an acknowledged write and its side effect?

**DSN · A.** Crash recovery, durable side effects and independent history checking fit dependability. The primary endpoint is orphan clinical writes per independent crash history after a fixed recovery window. Keep ambiguous outcomes in the denominator and measure recovery time. The strongest practical arm is a PostgreSQL atomic outbox with a genuinely idempotent consumer. Compare a real durable workflow engine such as Temporal and account for Beldi, Zhang et al.’s fault-tolerant transactional workflows (OSDI 2020), when defining the fault model. [DSN rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/787/) · [Registered estimands](venue2027/dsn/rq_and_estimands.yaml) · [Temporal](https://github.com/temporalio/sdk-python) · [Beldi](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi20/presentation/zhang-haoran)

**Evidence and decision.** Local synthetic crash histories exist. Beldi's published application workloads are an external integration target, with no completed CLARA recovery comparison established here. Map a workload to observable operation and delivery IDs and exercise real durable recovery at matched crash boundaries. First reproduce one history where the candidate changes the recovered outcome relative to a fully functioning outbox, or establish a prespecified recovery-cost advantage at equal correctness. DSN owns post-crash durable state. COMPSAC owns admission before a crash. If the baseline reconciles equally well at equal cost, stop the journal-mechanism paper. [Local inventory](venue2027/proof/artifacts/dsn_dataset/inventory.json) · [Upstream Beldi workloads](https://github.com/eniac/Beldi)

### 5. Replay-closed delegation — Does lineage add anything to single-use contextual credentials?

**IFIP SEC · B.** Multi-principal authorization under changing purpose and delegation fits applied security. Measure invalid admissions per unique oracle-denied replay history, paired with legitimate-control acceptance. Compare properly scoped Macaroons (Birgisson et al., NDSS 2014), a fresh relationship check grounded in Zanzibar, and Cedar or OPA plus an atomic consumption ledger. Give strong arms the same current policy, nonce storage and transaction budget wherever their semantics support them. [IFIP SEC rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/804/) · [Registered estimands](venue2027/ifip_sec/rq_and_estimands.yaml) · [Macaroons](https://research.google/pubs/macaroons-cookies-with-contextual-caveats-for-decentralized-authorization-in-the-cloud/) · [Zanzibar](https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc19/presentation/pang)

**Evidence and decision.** The local interleavings are synthetic. Public OpenFGA models can supply independently authored principal graphs. The consume-once oracle and timing still need separate case construction and labeling. Require a held-out multi-hop decision difference with valid controls on real concurrent sessions, or report equivalence. GLHS already found perfect decision concordance with its compact signed token. This paper needs new delegation chains, separate from PolicyTrace’s translation fixtures and GovRed’s single disclosure-to-commit schedules. [Local inventory](venue2027/proof/artifacts/ifip_sec_dataset/inventory.json) · [External model source](https://github.com/openfga/sample-stores) · [GLHS E16](glhs_journal/q4_r4/evidence/E16_five_way_comparator/derived/summary.md)

### 6. Attestation evidence in tool-using AI — Which published claims exceed the existing vocabulary?

**ACNS · B, conditional on a suitable paper category.** The contribution would be a reproducible systematization of attestation claims and trust assumptions. The primary quantity is the fraction of extracted claims requiring an additional evidence category, clustered by publication. The strong reference is the combined vocabulary of IETF RATS (RFC 9334) and W3C PROV-DM. Debenedetti et al.’s AgentDojo (NeurIPS 2024) and Ruan et al.’s ToolEmu (ICLR 2024) provide independent examples for checking the coding manual. [ACNS rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/907/) · [Registered estimands](venue2027/acns/rq_and_estimands.yaml) · [RATS](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9334) · [PROV-DM](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/)

**Evidence and decision.** The retrieval ledger covers eight of nine registered seeds. The five DBLP title queries identified 78 distinct keys for screening by 20 November. Independent screening decisions remain outstanding. [DBLP identification](venue2027/acns/corpus/DBLP_IDENTIFICATION.md) The W3C PROV-DM endpoint returned HTTP 403. Recover that seed through a traceable source before coding. Close the registered search window through December 2026, deduplicate the full corpus and measure pre-adjudication agreement and residual coverage against both standards. If two independent coders cannot reproduce a material residual, retain the taxonomy as documentation. The evidence units are external publications. GLHS dispatch traces and its E15–E22 cases cannot supply the numerator. [Seed receipts](venue2027/acns/corpus/seed_retrieval_receipts.json) · [Review protocol](venue2027/acns/design/sok_review_protocol.md)

### 7. Clinical workflow refresh — Can a selective context update preserve authorized, useful answers?

**AIME · B.** This asks whether clinical task completion survives a change in medication history or permission. Score unsafe actions per counterfactual vignette pair, separating action eligibility from a clinically material omission. Report unnecessary refusal. The strongest decision comparator is CLARA's full per-step refresh with current policy filtering on the same model and prompt. OpenAI’s HealthBench supplies physician-authored evaluation criteria. It is an evaluation reference, and its published scores cannot substitute for this matched arm. [AIME rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/151/) · [Registered estimands](venue2027/aime/rq_and_estimands.yaml) · [HealthBench](https://openai.com/index/healthbench/)

**Evidence and decision.** The local cases are synthetic. External HealthBench conversations are candidates for a source-disjoint base set only if the task and reuse conditions fit. Consent counterfactuals would be newly authored. Freeze independent clinical and policy labels and an adequate reviewed case set before comparing outputs across the planned two providers. Provider availability and blinded clinician adjudication are open dependencies. Without both axes, the results support at most a workflow pilot. PETS owns egress privacy. This study owns appropriateness and permitted completion. [Local inventory](venue2027/proof/artifacts/aime_dataset/inventory.json) · [Annotation rubric](venue2027/aime/design/clinical_annotation_rubric.md)

### 8. GovernanceTrace — Do ordered events improve detection at a fixed false-alarm budget?

**DIMVA · B.** Defensive evaluation of minimal audit telemetry fits intrusion detection. Measure campaign-level precision–recall area and recall at the registered benign false-alarm budget, holding out entire families and tenants. Compare tuned count and temporal-rule detectors, Isolation Forest, boosted trees and a sequence comparator grounded in Du et al.’s DeepLog (CCS 2017). Give every arm identical available features, history length and validation access. [DIMVA rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/565/) · [Registered estimands](venue2027/dimva/rq_and_estimands.yaml) · [DeepLog](https://doi.org/10.1145/3133956.3134015)

**Evidence and decision.** Local sessions are simulator-labeled. AgentDojo provides an external task suite, not independently labeled CLARA policy campaigns. Use only offline, labeled event records for a transfer test, verify independently that their labels and event types match, and report uncovered families. Hold out whole source templates and mask terminal reason codes. If gains disappear on source holdout or the temporal rule ties, drop the learned-detector claim. This study measures recognition from logs. It cannot reuse GovRed’s prevention outcomes or ReceiptMin’s reconstruction scores. [Local manifest](venue2027/proof/artifacts/dimva_dataset/manifest.json) · [AgentDojo](https://github.com/ethz-spylab/agentdojo)

### 9. ReceiptMin — How much investigative utility survives audit-data expiry?

**ACSAC · A, stretch.** A measured trade-off between forensic reconstruction and linkability under realistic retention privileges fits applied security. Primary endpoint is the fraction of frozen forensic questions answered exactly per history after expiry. Evaluate at matched linkability, audit-reader privileges and retention times. Compare full encrypted logs with role-gated retrieval and deletion, and an RFC 9162-style integrity log with authorized material retrieval. Schneier and Kelsey’s secure audit logs are the closest classical forensic design. The adaptations must disclose key and backup assumptions. [ACSAC rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/117/) · [Registered estimands](venue2027/pst/rq_and_estimands.yaml) · [Secure audit logs](https://www.schneier.com/academic/archives/1999/05/secure_audit_logs_to.html) · [RFC 9162](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9162)

**Evidence and decision.** Current cases use a local offline oracle. Independently generated HL7 AuditEvent examples could test field mappings, but example resources do not contain investigator questions, realistic longitudinal linkage distributions or independent answers. Secure a separate authorized, de-identified audit workload with those properties or keep the result explicitly synthetic. Test whether encryption with ordinary retention dominates minimized receipts. GLHS owns its existing forensic rubric and PETS owns future disclosure. This paper needs fresh post-expiry questions and linkage labels. [Local inventory](venue2027/proof/artifacts/pst_dataset/inventory.json) · [HL7 examples](https://hl7.org/fhir/R5/auditevent-examples.html)

### 10. CareGuard-VN — Can source-bound medication identity prevent false reassurance?

**AIME · B.** Medication identity is a clinically consequential intermediate decision in interaction screening. Estimate false clears over **all** frozen externally positive pairs, retaining identity failures in the denominator and reporting coverage to expose blanket abstention. Compare strict mapping with ordinary deterministic normalization on the same DDI checker and an oracle-identity arm. Korpela et al.’s RxMap is the closest named normalization system, currently asset-gated. CrossDDI (BioNLP 2026) is relevant to downstream verification. Its task starts from a different identity assumption. [AIME rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/151/) · [RxMap feasibility](careguard_vn/RXMAP_FEASIBILITY.md) · [CrossDDI](https://aclanthology.org/2026.bionlp-1.73/)

**Evidence and decision.** The project has controlled DDInter, DailyMed and RxNorm materials. The current Vietnamese product identity frame is missing. Obtain the authorized export, pass the four-source validation gate, then freeze product-disjoint assignments, variant clusters and mapping review before any scored run. Absence in DDInter cannot label a negative. An underpowered or missing positive reference leaves false-clear risk unquantified. This is the registry’s existing CareGuard study, counted once. AIME workflow vignettes and historical DrugBank conformance supply no cases. [Source and precision gates](careguard_vn/READINESS.md) · [Frozen statistics plan](careguard_vn/STATISTICS_PLAN_FROZEN.md)

### 11. Vietnamese Scribe — Can selective transcription catch meaning-changing errors at a fixed review budget?

**Interspeech · A, exploratory.** Study whether disagreement between decoders plus acoustic confidence identifies materially mistranscribed numbers, negation and named entities in Vietnamese speech. Prespecify the primary endpoint as the fraction of reference-marked spans with a meaning-changing error among automatically accepted spans at a fixed review fraction. Split and estimate uncertainty by speaker. Compare confidence-only and disagreement-only selection on the same pooled transcripts, using matched-compute multilingual Whisper (Radford et al., ICML 2023) and VinAI’s Vietnamese-adapted PhoWhisper. Report WER and true coverage alongside selective risk. [Interspeech rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/494/) · [CLARA ASR entry point](../services/asr/server.py) · [Whisper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/radford23a.html) · [PhoWhisper](https://github.com/VinAIResearch/PhoWhisper)

**Evidence and decision.** Google's public FLEURS Vietnamese split is a potential general-speech feasibility set. There is no linked CLARA scored span corpus or independently validated clinical dictation set for this proposal. First audit speaker identifiers, reference-span yield and overlap with model adaptation data. Freeze disjoint speakers, independently labeled spans and an equal review budget before decoding the holdout. If an independent corpus with sufficient target phenomena cannot be assembled, drop the candidate. A clinical Scribe claim would additionally require clinical dictation. CareGuard starts from drug strings and AIME from vignettes. This study starts from audio and scores transcription, without reusing either label set. [FLEURS dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs)

## How to decide what becomes a manuscript

The registry reserves GovRed’s disclosure-to-commit schedules, GovMut’s 45-mutant W8 corpus and GLHS’s governed-write evidence for their original studies. The two BigData companions share parent freezes and FMC is presentation-only. None adds to the count. GLHS E16’s exact signed token and full mechanism agreed on all 500 schedules. GovMut’s 720 executions are repeated observations across 45 mutants. A later study needs a new primary unit, oracle and evidence freeze before it can count any new result. [Publication registry](publication_registry.yaml) · [E16 comparison](glhs_journal/q4_r4/evidence/E16_five_way_comparator/derived/summary.md) · [GovMut readiness](assurance_soict/READINESS.md)

Use the evidence gates in order. For studies 1–3, establish comparator fidelity and freeze the external workload before another optimization pass. For 4–5, first test whether a correctly implemented established mechanism already explains the result. For 6–11, resolve corpus or label feasibility before paying for a full evaluation. Keep tuning on development data. Record the eligible denominator, missing comparator runs, uncertainty at the independent-unit level and the outcome against the strongest comparator. A tie, reversal or unavailable arm remains visible. The portfolio supports eleven decisions about experiments today. An independent frozen result is what would support each manuscript.
