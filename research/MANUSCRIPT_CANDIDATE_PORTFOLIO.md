## Historical eleven-study shortlist

Decision date 2026-11-21. The initial shortlist began with **PolicyTrace**, **optimistic admission**, and **future disclosure**. Each has an executable comparison that can already lose to a credible baseline. The three studies need, respectively, an independently authored temporal policy corpus, a held-out workload with independently specified dependencies, and an observed dispatch workload with independently labeled revocations. None of the eleven has established superiority on its proposed external primary test.

The first nine entries map to the [venue2027 designs](venue2027/README.md). Each links its registered estimands. CareGuard is a separately registered, blocked study. The Vietnamese speech study is a new proposal. Venue ranks below are conference ranks from the official ICORE 2026 records, checked by matching the acronym, ranking source and rank on the portal. They describe target selectivity, not acceptance forecasts, open calls or journal quartiles. SACMAT and PST both rank C, so their original destinations are outside the requested range. ## Candidate studies

### 1. PolicyTrace — Can temporal tests distinguish translation errors from policy disagreements?

**ICST · A.** A portable test oracle, cross-engine validity and equal-budget testing fit a software testing venue. The registered multiplicity-primary endpoint is the RQ2 mutation score at fixed oracle-call budget. RQ1 measures detection of conformance divergences on paired history/request units. Report unsupported semantics separately. Compare the temporal generator with stateful property-based generation and Xu et al.’s coverage-based XACML testing (SACMAT 2018), with the same oracle-call budget. The original XPA MC/DC generator has now been exercised on conference3; transfer of this comparator to histories and the equal-budget comparison remain open. Cedar, OpenFGA and OPA are systems under test. [ICST rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/1221/) · [Registered estimands](venue2027/sacmat/rq_and_estimands.yaml) · [Xu et al.](https://doi.org/10.1145/3205977.3205979)

**Evidence and decision.** On 24 real Cedar fixtures, honoring the declared fixture format gave 78/78 expected decisions. The legacy adapter had eight disagreements and two errors. That is direct evidence that adapter bugs can masquerade as findings. On one published temporal-access model, native OpenFGA and its OPA translation each reproduced seven upstream assertions and six additional exact-expiry controls. Treat the model as one external fixture. [Query-level results](venue2027/proof/artifacts/sacmat_external_temporal_parity_dev.json) Freeze upstream policy models with independently labeled ordered changes and require portable semantics before counting a divergence. A result confined to corrected parsing belongs in the adapter documentation. GovMut owns its W8 mutant scores. This paper needs fresh cross-engine histories and must survive removal of every W8 case. [External fixture result](venue2027/proof/artifacts/sacmat_cedar_external_dev.json) · [Temporal development corpus](venue2027/proof/artifacts/sacmat_temporal_corpus.json) · [Upstream OpenFGA sample stores](https://github.com/openfga/sample-stores)

### 2. Dependency-complete optimistic admission — When does a smaller read set help?

**COMPSAC · B.** A study of deriving application dependencies and measuring their cost fits software systems engineering. Measure false-stale rejection among oracle-allowed writes, conditional on the registered forbidden-commit safety gate. Compare against native PostgreSQL SERIALIZABLE executing all required predicates, global-version OCC and a clearly labeled Silo-style port. Silo is Tu et al. (SOSP 2013). PostgreSQL SSI is Ports and Grittner (PVLDB 2012). Use the publisher PDF below because the DOI in the local COMPSAC comparator matrix points to a different paper. [COMPSAC rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/871/) · [Registered margins and units](venue2027/compsac/rq_and_estimands.yaml) · [Silo](https://doi.org/10.1145/2517349.2522713) · [PostgreSQL SSI](https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol5/p1850_danrkports_vldb2012.pdf)

**Evidence and decision.** Real PostgreSQL 16.4 development rows compare lock strategies and SSI on shared dependencies. The inputs are local. Adapt a held-out transaction mix from CMU's BenchBase and have a separate specification derive its legal read sets. Report which added clinical predicates are synthetic. Freeze before runtime tuning. Require zero forbidden commits with the registered upper bound, then evaluate the prespecified ten-percentage-point false-stale reduction. Compare cost against SSI even if coarse OCC loses. GLHS already owns co-versioned commit safety. A speedup over an unnecessarily exclusive lock alone cannot support the new paper. [Five development rounds and seed-level results](venue2027/compsac/submission/pg_development_results.md) · [BenchBase](https://github.com/cmu-db/benchbase)

### 3. Future disclosure after revocation — Can derived caches match full recomputation at lower cost?

**PETS · A.** Privacy of future dispatches is the claim. Count independent subject histories with any forbidden attribute observed at egress after revocation. Compare transitive invalidation with a coarse purge and freshly recomputed authorized projection at every dispatch. Pang et al.’s Zanzibar (USENIX ATC 2019) supplies the consistency model for a further tagged-cache comparison. OpenFGA is an independent Zanzibar-inspired implementation. Cache tagging remains an adaptation. [PETS rank](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/1442/) · [Registered estimands](venue2027/popets/rq_and_estimands.yaml) · [Zanzibar](https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc19/presentation/pang)

**Evidence and decision.** The 12-case in-process pilot records incomplete arms and unverified semantic parity. A paired dispatch study subsequently observed all 185 requests per arm across nine histories. Three histories experienced revocation. In these histories both arms delivered all 384 authorized bytes and zero forbidden bytes. The reverse-index microbenchmark measures invalidation cost. [Observed dispatches](../artifacts/venue2027/popets/dispatch-dev-01/RESULTS.md) LongMemEval (Wu et al., ICLR 2025) offers independent longitudinal conversation structure for a proposed holdout. It lacks the required consent ground truth. Freeze a separate permission overlay, identify synthetic labels, and compare observed outgoing bytes and end-to-end cost, including index construction, on the same histories. If full refresh is equally private and inexpensive, retire the mechanism advantage. GLHS commit acceptance and historical provider erasure are outside this endpoint. [Pilot](venue2027/proof/artifacts/popets_pilot_report.json) · [Index measurements](venue2027/proof/artifacts/popets_scope_index_development.json) · [LongMemEval](https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval)

**External development result.** Across 128 dialogues from one Schema-Guided Dialogue training shard, all three arms delivered every authorized field and zero forbidden bytes under a synthetic midpoint withdrawal. B4 served 305 more authorized hits than B1 (74.55% versus 67.82%), but median process wall time was 159.9 ms for B4, 110.9 ms for B1 and 105.7 ms for B2. This supports selective cache retention for repeated state reads. Cheap local reconstruction favored B2 on elapsed time. The longitudinal holdout and registered density comparison remain open. [Paired external replay and limitations](../artifacts/venue2027/popets/sgd-dev-01/RESULTS.md)

### 4. Recoverable safety journals — Can recovery reconcile an acknowledged write and its side effect?

## How to decide what becomes a manuscript

The registry reserves GovRed’s disclosure-to-commit schedules, GovMut’s 45-mutant W8 corpus and GLHS’s governed-write evidence for their original studies. The two BigData companions share parent freezes and FMC is presentation-only. None adds to the count. GLHS E16’s exact signed token and full mechanism agreed on all 500 schedules. GovMut’s 720 executions are repeated observations across 45 mutants. A later study needs a new primary unit, oracle and evidence freeze before it can count any new result. [Publication registry](publication_registry.yaml) · [E16 comparison](glhs_journal/q4_r4/evidence/E16_five_way_comparator/derived/summary.md) · [GovMut readiness](assurance_soict/READINESS.md)

Use the evidence gates in order. For studies 1–3, establish comparator fidelity and freeze the external workload before another optimization pass. For 4–5, first test whether a correctly implemented established mechanism already explains the result. For 6–11, resolve corpus or label feasibility before paying for a full evaluation. Keep tuning on development data. Record the eligible denominator, missing comparator runs, uncertainty at the independent-unit level and the outcome against the strongest comparator. A tie, reversal or unavailable arm remains visible. The portfolio supports eleven decisions about experiments today. An independent frozen result is what would support each manuscript.
